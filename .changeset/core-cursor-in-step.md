The cursor stays in step. Calls that overlap share one hand: the later call takes it and the earlier one finishes without a press, instead of both pressing mid-flight. A press lands on the frame that draws it, waits under way end when the person takes over, and the ring of a control pressed while the person was busy follows the page. A password is never read out (`fill` carries `"••••"`), and status is announced by the tool's title. `cursor.css`: `--agent` alone recolours the agent (the other colours derive from it), a new `--agent-edge` keeps the ring visible on dark pages, the ring no longer depends on the page's `box-sizing`, and the spotlight follows `--agent-z`. `locate` reads a name without `aria-hidden` and `hidden` parts and with an image's `alt`; a parameter's field by `name` is always a native field.
